# Maven Fuzzy Factory — Key Insights & Strategic Recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights

**1. The Channel Paradox — Volume vs. Value**
Google Paid is the business's volume engine, driving 0.32M sessions and $1.28M in revenue — by far the largest channel. But SocialBook, despite tiny volume, converts the highest-value customers: its Average Order Value ($64.90) beats every other channel, suggesting it attracts a smaller pool of high-intent, premium buyers rather than casual browsers.

**2. Desktop Dominance & the Office-Hours Pattern**
86.05% of orders come from desktop. Traffic across all five weekdays (Monday–Friday) clusters tightly around 13,700–14,300 sessions at peak hours, with essentially no single day standing out — but weekend volume (Saturday–Sunday) collapses to roughly a third of that level. This points to a consistent "browse and buy from the office" behavior on weekdays, not a specific Monday/Friday effect. A likely driver: this looks less like planned shopping and more like employees taking a quick mental break during the workday — browsing (and occasionally buying) from their desktop as a short escape from work — which fits why the pattern repeats evenly across every weekday rather than spiking on one particular day.

**3. The "Birthday Sugar Panda" Dilemma**
This product delivers the highest profit margin in the portfolio (68.49%) but also carries the highest refund rate (6.04%) — well above the 4.32% company average. The same product that protects margin the most is also the one costing the business the most in returns, signalling a possible quality or expectation-setting gap.

**4. The Real Funnel Leak: Product Detail → Cart**
The single biggest drop in the entire purchase funnel happens between the product detail page and the cart step — a 54.8% relative loss (44.45% → 20.08%), more than double the drop-off at any other transition, including checkout/billing. This is where the business is losing the most potential customers, and it's the step most worth fixing first.

**5. A/B Testing Success — Billing Page Redesign**
The redesigned billing page delivered a 37.3% uplift in conversion (45.66% → 62.69%) during testing — clear proof that a UX fix at this stage translates directly into revenue, and validates rolling it out further.

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan

**1. Fix the Add-to-Cart Leak**
- **Goal:** Recover the single largest source of lost customers in the funnel.
- **Action:** Investigate the product detail page — pricing clarity, shipping-cost visibility, trust signals, and the prominence of the "Add to Cart" call-to-action. Prioritize an A/B test on this step before any other funnel investment, since it has the largest quantified upside.
- **Impact:** [X]% improvement in Product Detail → Cart conversion = [Y] more orders/month = +$[Z] monthly revenue *(figure to be calculated from the actual session/order data before this goes in the deck)*

**2. Roll Out the Winning Billing Page Company-Wide**
- **Goal:** Lock in the proven 37.3% conversion lift.
- **Action:** Apply the winning billing-page design across all remaining traffic that hasn't yet been migrated, and monitor conversion rate post-rollout to confirm the lift holds at scale.

**3. Product Quality Audit — Birthday Sugar Panda**
- **Goal:** Protect the portfolio's strongest margin product from being undermined by returns.
- **Action:** Investigate the manufacturing quality and product description accuracy for this SKU to bring its 6.04% refund rate down toward the product-line average.

**4. Mobile Experience Optimization**
- **Goal:** Grow the mobile share of orders beyond its current 13.95%, and close the weekend traffic gap.
- **Action:** Total weekend traffic drops to roughly a third of weekday levels. A plausible contributing factor is that users away from their desktops on weekends default to mobile, and friction in the mobile experience discourages them from browsing or buying — alongside the simpler explanation that people are just less inclined to shop on days off. Audit the mobile checkout flow specifically for friction (form length, load speed, tap targets) to close the gap with desktop conversion, and treat this as a hypothesis worth validating with mobile-specific weekend conversion data going forward.

**5. Maintain Server Capacity During Peak Traffic Windows**
- **Goal:** Prevent downtime or slow load times during the highest-traffic hours, when the cost of an outage is greatest.
- **Action:** Ensure server/infrastructure capacity is provisioned to comfortably handle the identified weekday peak windows (late morning and early afternoon), with monitoring and auto-scaling in place so a traffic spike never turns into lost sales.

**6. Lifecycle & Retention Marketing**
- **Goal:** Keep the brand top-of-mind for the 394K unique visitors already acquired, and increase revenue per customer without added acquisition spend.
- **Action:** Deploy automated email/SMS campaigns around seasonal moments, and feature data-backed cross-sell pairs at checkout and in post-purchase emails — starting with the top pairing, The Original Mr. Fuzzy + The Hudson River Mini Bear.
